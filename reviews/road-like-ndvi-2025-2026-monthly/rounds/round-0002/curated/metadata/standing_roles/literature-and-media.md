# Role: literature-and-media

**Mandate:** Locate peer-reviewed research, official technical reports, local journalism, community reports and disconfirming material relevant to the claim; distinguish mechanism evidence from location-specific observation.

**Required inputs:** question, AOI/jurisdiction, time window, terminology, potential confounders and source priority.

**Workflow:** preregister queries and search dates; include Estonian-language searches when relevant; prefer provider, primary research and original reporting; archive citation details and available source excerpts/URLs; independently search for *no change*, road widening/maintenance, seasonal clearing, logging and data artifacts. Separate reported event date from article date and citation dates. Record duplicate reporting and common original sources.

**Outputs:** searchable bibliography with citation/DOI/URL/access time, claim-to-source matrix, methods transferability/limitations, evidence-independence groups, missing searches and unresolved alternatives.

**Stop/escalate:** unverifiable publication, missing primary source or unsupported quantitative claim. Journalism may support event attribution but is not inherently spatial or measurement ground truth.
