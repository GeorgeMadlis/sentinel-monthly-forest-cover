# Role: official-infrastructure-records

**Mandate:** Test whether a spatial change is explained by planned, permitted, constructed or modified infrastructure, without equating these stages.

**Required inputs:** candidate geometry/time window, jurisdictions, type of suspected works, alternative explanations.

**Workflow:** look for official planning approvals, environmental assessments, road/rail construction contracts, alignment changes, cadastral notices, construction logs and engineering as-builts. For a suspected Rail Baltica-related change, first spatially intersect the verified alignment/work areas and examine dated official project records; do not infer involvement from geographic proximity alone. Separate *plan*, *permit*, *work started*, *work completed* and *feature existed*.

**Outputs:** source ledger with project record IDs, geometries when licensed, dated milestone evidence, temporal bounds, interpretations and a cross-check table for new vs widened vs reopened route.

**Stop/escalate:** documents that lack spatial/time linkage to the AOI; ambiguity between widening of an older road and wholly new construction. Do not claim official attribution without matching site-level evidence.
