# JRC Tropical Moist Forest (TMF)

Independent forest/non-forest and degradation reference for humid tropics.

Annual forest / non-forest classification for the humid tropics. Independent of Hansen GFC - useful for cross-validation. Includes deforestation / degradation classes.

Inventory access routes are historical assessments; select and verify provider, version, coverage and access at planning time. Individual scenes are excluded.
