# Sentinel-1 GRD (C-band SAR)

Cloud-robust SAR signal for forest structure and monthly disturbance confirmation.

Cloud-robust v2 complement to Sentinel-2. VV+VH polarizations. Requires speckle filtering and terrain correction. Backscatter sensitive to moisture and structure.

Inventory access routes are historical assessments; select and verify provider, version, coverage and access at planning time. Individual scenes are excluded.
