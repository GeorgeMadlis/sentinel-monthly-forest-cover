# Sentinel-2 MSI L2A

High-resolution optical signal for forest state and disturbance confirmation.

Core v2 input. 10 m optical. Cloud masking required. Monthly compositing with SCL or s2cloudless. v3: usable as input to weakly supervised model.

Inventory access routes are historical assessments; select and verify provider, version, coverage and access at planning time. Individual scenes are excluded. NDVI is derived from reflectance bands, not asserted to be a native dataset band.
