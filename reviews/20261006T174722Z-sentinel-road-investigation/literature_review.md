# Literature and threshold interpretation

Optical visibility and a radar decrease are different requirements. A narrow bright surface or cleared verge can alter optical reflectance while occupying only part of a 20 m analysis pixel. Radoux’s separability framework and Oehmcke’s road models support width/contrast-dependent visibility, but do not validate this NDVI/SAR gate [L01, L02]. Apparent sampled widths are only visual estimates, and archived optical source windows are already resampled from nominal 10 m bands to 20 m. No universal minimum detectable width is claimed.

ESA explains how moisture, surface roughness, polarization and incidence influence radar returns [L05]. Maintained/increased backscatter after clearing is therefore a plausible alternative to a VV/VH drop. The actual local moisture state was not measured. The radar summer median and one June optical image describe different temporal summaries. Scene-level RTC compatibility and common orbit do not establish exact local co-registration; the issue report L07 is a caution from another location, not a diagnosed defect here.

CCDC explicitly treats geometric registration and atmosphere as prerequisites, combines temporal screening with repeated observations, and documents loss of useful clear pixels when selecting only clear scenes [L04, Methods]. This supports checking clear-scene sampling bias; it does not calibrate our eight-date reference. BFAST separates trend and seasonality using a longer series [L03, Methods]. Neither framework was run on this inadequate series. The three-date experiment is a separate heuristic, not either published algorithm or the Lab’s consecutive-month forest-confirmation method.

The supplied MAD helper was executed for all 18 declared combinations. Median/MAD reduces the influence of some reference outliers, while the minimum drop avoids flagging arbitrarily tiny effects when variability is near zero. The normal-consistency multiplier in L08 is not evidence of a normal NDVI error distribution. Five reference dates, epsilon={.02,.05}, effect sizes {.1,.15,.2} and k={2,2.5,3} remain unvalidated experimental choices. Without held-out independent road labels, smaller areas and more persistence are stability diagnostics, not precision/recall, significance, probability or false-discovery control. All settings were retained; none was selected for looking most like a road.

Paired-valid AND, OR and disagreement were generated using the repository fusion functions. AND is restrictive by construction; OR admits signals from either sensor and can increase false positives as well as recall. No probabilistic fusion was fitted because independent labels and calibration/holdout design are absent. The Congo abstract [L09] points to a different supervised workflow; it cannot justify transferring its results.

- [L01: Sentinel-2’s Potential for Sub-Pixel Landscape Feature Detection](https://www.mdpi.com/2072-4292/8/6/488) (2016): indexed original abstract/discussion/conclusion excerpts; direct full-text fetch returned 429. Abstract, Discussion, Conclusions Subpixel visibility depends on contrast and spatial response. Reported road limit is context-specific. Does not establish an NDVI anomaly cutoff or a universal road-width detection limit.
- [L02: Detecting Hardly Visible Roads in Low-Resolution Satellite Time Series Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.05026) (2019): full original author paper text read. II-A, III, IV and Appendix Visibility varies with road type/width; labels and temporal context influence extraction. OSM label noise/domain shift and supervised setup differ from this unsupervised gate.
- [L03: Detecting trend and seasonal changes in satellite image time series](https://otext.robjhyndman.com/publications/bfast1/) (2010): author bibliographic page and original-author preprint methods/conclusion read. Preprint 2, 5, 6 Separates temporal components; change characterization still needs causal interpretation. Not executable defensibly with eight reference dates and three June targets; not a road detector.
- [L04: Continuous change detection and classification of land cover using all available Landsat data](https://gerslab.cahnr.uconn.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/2514/2021/06/ZheZhu_CCDC.pdf) (2014): author-hosted journal full text read. 2, 3.1–3.3, printed pp.154–157 Single-date ephemeral effects and clear-scene selection can undermine inference; uses repeated dates. Published temporal model and data requirements differ from the June persistence heuristic; thresholds not transferable.
- [L05: Some Basic Radar Principles (SP-1199)](https://www.esa.int/esapub/sp/sp1199/get21.htm) (1996): official full text read. backscatter factors and polarization/incidence paragraphs Moisture, roughness, polarization and incidence affect backscatter; a wet or rough surface need not exhibit a drop. Explains mechanisms, not actual local weather or SAR cause.
- [L06: Earth Search documentation and offset issue #66](https://github.com/Element84/earth-search/blob/main/README.md) (2026): provider repository documentation and issue body read. collection/calibration documentation, issue body Collection metadata and offset behavior need product-specific checks; issue is a user report. Does not independently validate this run’s explicit gain=.0001, no-second-offset interpretation.
- [L07: Sentinel-1 RTC — step changes in terrain correction/projection #404](https://github.com/microsoft/PlanetaryComputer/issues/404) (2025): original issue body read; user report, not provider-confirmed general defect. description/reproduction Reported persistent geolocation shift in another region. Reason to check local registration, not evidence that this Estonia run has a shift. Optical checks do not certify S1 alignment.
- [L08: Alternatives to the Median Absolute Deviation](https://wis.kuleuven.be/stat/robust/papers/publications-1993/rousseeuwcroux-alternativestomedianad-jasa-1993.pdf) (1993): original author-hosted indexed abstract excerpt; full-text open failed. abstract MAD with a normal-consistency multiplier is robust; small-sample behavior still matters. Does not validate the noise floors, minimum count, effect-size cutoff or false-positive control for NDVI.
- [L09: Monitoring road development in Congo Basin forests with multi-sensor satellite imagery and deep learning](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0034425724004061) (2024): original publisher search abstract only; full text unavailable.  Road monitoring reported with an independently evaluated supervised model. Methods/labels not fully read; cannot justify exact thresholds or transfer accuracy to Estonia.
