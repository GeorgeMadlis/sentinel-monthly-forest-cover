Adjacent validation question for forest-disturbance interpretation: establish physical line identity using sufficiently resolved, dated, unobscured independent before/after imagery, spatial alignment and official records. Separate road existence, construction, reopening/widening, prior forest and legal attribution. Missing maps/masks are weak negative evidence. Conflicting acquisition metadata prevents interval inference. Geometry/width and source independence must be evaluated explicitly. Contradictions and limitations: the current imagery has date conflicts, no independent after pair, and railway/forestry-track alternatives. Intended as a human-review candidate; local ROI claims remain evidence rather than canonical concepts.
